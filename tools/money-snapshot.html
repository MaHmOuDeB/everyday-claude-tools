Data on this page

Your file never leaves this page. It is read here, in your browser. It is only sent anywhere if you press the tips button, and then only totals go out: category totals, repeating payments and the date range.

Saving tips from Claude

Claude sees only your category totals, your repeating payments (name and amount) and the date range. It never sees the file or single payments. Untick a repeating payment above to keep it out.

Show exactly what is sent

Money Snapshot sorts payments with simple word rules, so some will land in the wrong category. It is a rough picture, not financial advice. If money is tight or you owe a lot, a free debt advice service can help.
