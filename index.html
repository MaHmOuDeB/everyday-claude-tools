Everyday Claude Tools

Small tools for everyday paperwork and life admin

Seven free pages that help you read a letter, check a contract, plan dinner, prepare a call, look at your money and make a choice. Each answers in 12 languages.

How it works

  1. Open a tool on claude.ai while you are signed in.
  2. Each answer uses your own Claude account. Nobody else pays or sees it.
  3. The pages here work in Copy-as-prompt mode: they copy the request, and you paste it into any Claude chat.

  • Letter Helper

    Paste or photograph an official letter. See what it wants, the deadline and a reply you can send.

    Open on ClaudeTry without sign-in

  • Fridge to Dinner

    Photograph your fridge or list what you have. Get three dinners that fit your time and diet, or a week plan.

    Open on ClaudeTry without sign-in

  • Contract Checker

    Paste a phone, energy, gym or insurance contract. See what you really pay and the last day to cancel.

    Open on ClaudeTry without sign-in

  • Call Prep

    Prepare a phone call in a language you are learning. Get your opening line, phrases and a practice call.

    Open on ClaudeTry without sign-in

  • Money Snapshot

    Load a bank statement CSV. The page adds up money in and out and finds subscriptions. Claude sees only totals and the repeating payments you tick, and only if you ask.

    Open on ClaudeTry without sign-in

  • Say It Simply

    Turn a hard text into plain words. Or write what you want to say and get a clear, polite message.

    Open on ClaudeTry without sign-in

  • Decision Helper

    Stuck between two to four choices? Answer a few questions and see your options scored side by side.

    Open on ClaudeTry without sign-in

Privacy and limits

Nothing is stored on a server by these pages. Your text and photos go to Claude on your own account, only when you press the button. Do not paste ID numbers, bank details or passwords.

These tools explain and help you prepare. They are not legal, financial, medical or tax advice, and Claude can be wrong. Check anything important with a professional.
